## 01_bare_call.py :: # A Session with everything turned off is just a model call
### Three switches, and the lesson is the middle one
until: remember=False, max_tokens=400)
`remember=False` is the entire subject of this lesson. The transcript is still
recorded — it is simply never sent back. Lesson 02 changes this one word and
nothing else.

`tier="legacy"` picks a genuinely old model rather than a good one with its hands
tied: Llama 3 8B from April 2024, with no tool support and an 8,192-token window.
You will run into both of those limits on purpose before Part 1 is over.

`max_tokens=400` is a cap on the **answer**, not on the window. Keep the two
apart; the knowledge check at the bottom of this page is about exactly that
confusion.

## 01_bare_call.py :: # The one line that is the whole of "AI"
### This block is the whole of "calling an AI"
until: chat.send(QUESTION)
Two comments and one method call. One HTTPS request: a list of messages goes up,
one response comes back, and the connection closes. There is no session on the
other end, nothing is stored, and the model has no way to recognise you next time.

Everything else in this workshop — memory, tools, agents, the harness — is code on
**your** side of that request. Which is exactly why it is code you can audit.

## 01_bare_call.py :: ui.note(f'Now try a follow-up
### Watch this fail before you are told why
until: There is nothing to refer to.
The script prints the follow-up for the room to type, and then prints the answer
in advance, because the failure is the lesson. What comes back will be some
version of "I'm sorry, you haven't said anything yet" — and the footer will show
`history: dropped`.

Nothing is broken. Two separate requests were made, and the second one did not
include the first. Forgetting is the default behaviour of a language model; the
remembering you are used to is a feature somebody wrote.

## 01_bare_call.py :: # The chatbox. Same call, over and over
### The chatbox, so you can disprove it yourself
until: session.chat(chat)
Every lesson ends in these two lines: a prompt where you type your own messages
instead of the scripted ones. Try to get this model to recall something you told
it. You cannot, and ten seconds of trying is worth more than a paragraph of
explanation.

`/context` shows exactly what is being sent. `/help` lists the rest.
