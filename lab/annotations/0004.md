## agent.py :: # THE GATE. Three lines, and they are the thesis of Part 1:
### The gate: three lines, and the thesis of Part 1
until: output = (execute or execute_tool)(block.name, block.input)
Read the ownership carefully, because this is the sentence to take to a customer.
The model decides what it would *like* to happen. `tools.needs_approval` decides
whether that is sensitive. `ui.approve` draws the prompt. **This `if`/`elif`/`else`**
— not the model — decides whether anything runs at all.

Nothing the model can say reaches this decision. An agent is safe because of what
its harness permits, not because of how its prompt is worded.

## agent.py :: # The iteration cap. A guardrail against a loop that never converges
### The cap nobody thinks about until it saves them
until: MAX_ITERATIONS = 20
A model that keeps asking for one more file will keep asking until your budget or
your context window is gone. Twenty is arbitrary; having a number is not.

Set it to 2 and re-run the lesson to see what a too-tight cap costs — the
investigation stops mid-thought, with a confident partial answer.
